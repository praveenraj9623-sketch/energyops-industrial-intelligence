# Scope and interpretation limits

This is a historical 2018 steel-industry energy dataset, not a live feed or real-time monitoring system. It has no machine identifiers, production volume or output, tariff, incident labels, or verified CO₂ unit/methodology. It cannot establish machine failures, OEE, energy waste, savings, or carbon reduction.

The timestamps have no supplied timezone or daylight-saving metadata. Calendar labels and 15-minute continuity are internally consistent, but the absolute instant cannot be established from the CSV alone. The `Load_Type` classification method is unknown. Future baselines and rules must state eligibility and investigate deviations, not present them as proven incidents.
